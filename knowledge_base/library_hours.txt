# Library Operating Hours and Holidays

## Standard Operating Hours
- **Monday to Thursday:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Saturday and Sunday:** 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- **Friday & Gazetted Holidays:** Closed

## Section-Specific Timings
- **Reception and Internet Section:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- **Membership Registration:** 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM (Monday to Thursday)

## Lunch Breaks
- **Standard (Mon-Thu):** 1:00 PM - 2:00 PM (1300 - 1400 hrs)
- **Friday:** 12:30 PM - 2:00 PM (1230 - 1400 hrs)

## After-Hours Returns
Books can be returned after operating hours using the secure drop-box located at the main entrance. Books returned via the drop-box after closing will be processed on the next business day.
